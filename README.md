What the Website is For
- To show customers what bread and cakes we sell.
- To let customers send a cake enquiry online instead of phoning.
- To show our address, hours and contact details clearly.

What is on the Website
- 5 pages: Home, About Us, Our Products, Enquire, and Contact Us.
- Pink and cream colours to make it look warm and friendly.
- A form where people can order custom cakes.
- Social media icons at the bottom (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp).
- A "Top" button that appears when you scroll down.
- The site works on phones, tablets and computers.

Pages on the Site
- **Home** (index.html): The front page with a welcome message and a picture of a cake.
- **About Us** (about.html): The story of the bakery, our mission and vision.
- **Our Products** (services.html): The bread, cupcakes and cakes we sell with prices.
- **Enquire** (enquiry.html): A form to order a custom cake.
- **Contact Us** (contact.html): Our address, opening hours, phone and email.
